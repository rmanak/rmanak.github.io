I am currently a senior applied scientist in Amazon. Previous, I worked as a senior data scientist at Lyft
focusing on applications of NLP and machine learning in community and road safety, support as well as
applications of deep learning in spatio-temporal demand forecasting. Before Lyft, I was the co-founder and
chief data scientist in [Aida][aida], a machine learning startup, where I helped with research and
development of AI solutions for natural language understanding in customer support/intelligent segment.
Our company was [acquired by Vision Critical][vc] in late 2017.

Before working in industry, I was a PhD student in computational physics at University of British Columbia,
where I worked on large scale distributed computing and numerical algorithms for gravitational physics
research [[+]][ubc]. My main interest these days is machine learning particularly focused on natural
language understanding and more recently I am learning about meta-learning and how/why LLMs showcasing
few-shot learning capabilities. I am finishing the AI graduate program at Stanford. In my free time, I do
[landscape photography][photo], DIY projects and traveling.
